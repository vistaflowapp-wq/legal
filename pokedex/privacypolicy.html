Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 10/05/2026
Last Updated: 10/05/2026

Welcome to the Pocketdex companion App, developed by NextGenIT devs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application to explore Creature data, TCG cards, and battle statistics.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The CreatureDexApp is designed to be used without requiring a personal account or registration. However, we collect certain data to provide features like premium status and crash reporting.

A. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type, model, and operating system version.
  • App usage statistics (e.g., which Creature or TCG sets are viewed most).
  • Crash logs and diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • General location data (IP address) to serve relevant advertisements.

B. In-App Purchases (Subscriptions)

If you purchase CreatureDexPlus or CreatureDexPro, transactions are processed via the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not store your credit card information; however, we store a persistent "Premium" flag and your purchased Plan ID on your device to unlock features.

C. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to free users. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising IDs (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • App interaction data and ad performance metrics.

For more details, review Google’s Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide Creature data via PokeAPI and TCG data.
  • To manage and restore Premium subscriptions.
  • To display localized content based on your device language.
  • To analyze app performance and fix technical bugs.
  • To provide the "Daily Quiz" feature and track completion locally.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party providers to enhance your experience:

  • PokeAPI: Provides the core Creature data and sprites.
  • Creature TCG API: Provides trading card data and images.
  • Firebase: Used for configuration management and crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob: Used for advertising services.

4. Data Retention

We retain your "Favorites" and "Premium status" locally on your device. If you uninstall the App, this local data may be deleted. Premium status can be restored using the "Restore Purchase" feature within the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The CreatureDexApp is a general audience application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. All data used (Creature names, stats, etc.) is public information provided by the cited APIs.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
