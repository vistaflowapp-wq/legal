Welcome to FaithQuest - Bible Reader. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Your Privacy Matters:
FaithQuest is committed to providing a safe, secure, and inspiring Bible reading experience while respecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use FaithQuest, we may collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version).
  • Anonymous analytics and usage statistics.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
  • Bookmarks, favorites, notes, and reading preferences.
  • Reading progress and streak information.
  • Subscription status for premium features (if applicable).

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide Bible reading and study features.
  • Save bookmarks, favorites, and notes.
  • Track reading progress and daily streaks.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.
  • Fix bugs and technical issues.
  • Protect against misuse and maintain security.

FaithQuest may display advertisements through Google AdMob or other advertising partners.

Advertising providers may collect:

Advertisements may be personalized depending on your consent settings and applicable laws.
