Welcome to PuzzleHub, developed by NextGenIT devs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

By using PuzzleHub, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

PuzzleHub does not require account creation or registration. However, certain information may be collected automatically to improve app performance and user experience.

A. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • App usage analytics and performance metrics.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • General device information required for app functionality.

B. Advertising Information

PuzzleHub may display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device information.
  • Ad interaction and performance data.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

C. Local Game Data

PuzzleHub stores game progress, statistics, scores, records, and settings locally on your device.

Examples include:

  • Sudoku progress and statistics.
  • 2048 scores and best records.
  • Water Sort game progress.
  • Minesweeper statistics.
  • Number Match records and achievements.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve gameplay features.
  • To save your game statistics and preferences.
  • To monitor app stability and fix bugs.
  • To display advertisements within the application.
  • To improve performance and user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

PuzzleHub may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics - App usage analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics - Crash reporting and diagnostics.
  • Google AdMob - Advertising services.
  • Google Play Services - Essential platform functionality.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Most game-related data is stored locally on your device.

If you use the "Reset Data" option within the app settings, your game progress, statistics, and saved records may be permanently deleted.

Uninstalling the application may also remove locally stored data.

5. Children's Privacy

PuzzleHub is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect collected information and maintain the security of our services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
